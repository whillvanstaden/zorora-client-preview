Privacy policy

ZORORA ONE STOP (PTY) LTD respects your privacy.

Information you provide

When you enquire, you may provide your name, surname, contact number, email address, product requirements and delivery address.

How we use your information

We use your information only to contact you about your enquiry, prepare quotes, arrange collection or delivery, and provide relevant product, pricing and availability information. Promotional product updates are sent only with your consent or where permitted by law. You can ask us to stop these updates at any time.

Sharing and keeping information

We do not sell your personal information. It is shared only where needed to respond to your request or arrange supply, with service providers assisting us, or where legally required. Website enquiries are processed through FormSubmit to deliver them to our inbox. We take reasonable steps to protect it and keep it only as long as needed for these purposes or legal requirements.

Your choices and contact

You may request access to, correction or deletion of your personal information, subject to applicable legal requirements, or opt out of product updates. Email admin@zorora.co.za or call +27 66 471 5862.

You may also raise a privacy complaint with the Information Regulator of South Africa.
